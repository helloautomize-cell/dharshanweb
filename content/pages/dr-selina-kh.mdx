### About
Dr. Selina Kh is a physiatrist, a doctor who specialises in physical medicine and rehabilitation. She looks after people recovering from stroke and spinal cord injury, people living with joint and rheumatic conditions, and people with long-term pain.

Her care is built around the person, not just the diagnosis. She explains what is happening, supports you through the hard parts of recovery and helps you get back your confidence as well as your movement.

### How Dr. Selina works
- **Plain explanations.** Scans, options and next steps in simple language.
- **One team.** She works with therapists and other doctors on one plan.

### Care she looks after
Stroke rehabilitation · Spinal cord injury rehabilitation · Spasticity care · Back, neck and joint pain · Rheumatology-related pain
