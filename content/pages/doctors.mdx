### Intro
Every plan at Rehabify is led by a physiatrist, a doctor trained in physical medicine and rehabilitation. Choose a doctor below, or book with no preference and the team will suggest the right one for you.

### Who to see
Pick the closest match. We show which doctor usually leads that care. Either doctor can see you first and bring in the other when it helps.

### How our doctors work
Both doctors work the same way: they find what is stopping you, agree on what you want back, and lead one plan with your therapists.

### Goals first
Every plan starts from what you want to be able to do again, such as walking to the market or sleeping without pain.

### Explained plainly
Scans, options and next steps in simple words, so you can decide with the doctor, not after them.

### One team
Your physiatrist leads, and works with physiotherapists and other therapists on one written plan.

### Your physiatrist
Finds the cause of pain or weakness
Orders and reads your scans
Prescribes medicines and splints
Does image-guided procedures when suitable

### Your physiotherapist
Teaches and guides your exercises
Gives hands-on treatment
Checks your home programme
Reports progress back to your doctor

### FAQs
1. **Can I see either doctor?** Yes. If you are not sure who to see, choose "No preference" in the booking form and the team will suggest a doctor based on your reason for visiting.
2. **Do I need a referral?** No. You can book directly. Bring any scans, reports or hospital discharge summary to a clinic visit, or share them before a video consult.
3. **Can I see a doctor by video?** Yes, for first advice, follow-ups and home programmes. If you need an examination or a procedure, the doctor will ask you to come in.
4. **Do you see children?** Yes. Dr. Dharshan looks after children with cerebral palsy, muscular dystrophy and developmental delay. A parent or guardian attends every consultation.

### Booking
Pick Dr. Dharshan, Dr. Selina or No preference. The team confirms your time by phone or WhatsApp.
